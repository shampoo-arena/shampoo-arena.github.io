Hair type

Fine hair, oily roots

Hair that goes limp and greasy quickly; needs cleansing without weight.

What the judge looks for

Favour effective but not stripping cleansing: moderate anionic surfactants, or gentler ones in sufficient concentration to actually remove sebum. Penalise heavy occlusives, rich butters, high-molecular-weight silicones and heavy cationic conditioning agents that weigh fine hair down or build up at the roots. Light humectants and protein are fine and often helpful for body.
