Dandruff and flaking scalp

Visible flaking, typically Malassezia-driven seborrhoeic dermatitis.

What the judge looks for

Weight heavily toward recognised antifungal or antimicrobial actives (for example zinc pyrithione, piroctone olamine, climbazole, selenium sulfide, ketoconazole, coal tar) and toward keratolytics such as salicylic acid. A product with no active at all should rank poorly here regardless of how pleasant the rest of the formula is. Penalise heavy fatty esters and oils that can feed Malassezia.
