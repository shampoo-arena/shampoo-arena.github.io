Hair type

Hair loss and shedding

Increased shedding or thinning density, where scalp condition and follicle support matter more than how the hair feels.

What the judge looks for

Favour ingredients with plausible mechanisms for shedding: scalp-stimulating and vasodilatory actives (caffeine, niacinamide, ginger, rosemary, menthol), anti-androgenic or DHT-related botanicals (saw palmetto / Serenoa serrulata, pumpkin seed, Trifolium pratense), peptides and amino acids, biotin and panthenol, and anti-inflammatory or antifungal agents that address a scalp environment implicated in shedding (piroctone olamine, zinc pyrithione, salicylic acid). Favour gentle cleansing that does not aggravate an irritated scalp. Penalise harsh sulfates high in the list, drying alcohols, and heavy occlusives that can build up around follicles. Note that a shampoo has limited contact time, so rate confidence accordingly rather than overstating what any rinse-off product can do; judge only what the ingredients support.
