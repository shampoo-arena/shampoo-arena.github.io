    • Formulation A is not without redeeming features: Hydroxypropyl Guar Hydroxypropyltrimonium Chloride and Polyquaternium-7 are genuine cationic conditioning polymers that will deposit on hair and partially offset the harsh surfactant, and Sodium Lauroyl Sarcosinate is a relatively mild co-anionic. Cocamidopropyl Betaine further moderates the system. However, these sit behind an olefin sulfonate primary, so the overall wash is still comparatively stripping.
    • Minor notes: A's salicylic acid and glycolic acid (both likely under 1%) hint at a mild keratolytic/clarifying lean - not decisive at those levels. B's benzyl alcohol is a preservative, not a drying alcohol at typical use levels, so it does not trigger the alcohol penalty. Neither formulation contains a chelator (no EDTA or equivalent) nor a UV filter, so those criteria do not differentiate them.
    • Neither is a perfect colour-care formulation (no chelation, no UV protection, B has no cationic conditioning agents), but the difference in surfactant harshness is clear-cut: A's base actively works against the priorities; B's base is built for minimal stripping.
