A's moisture package (Glycerin, Panthenol/Panthenyl Ethyl Ether, Sodium Hyaluronate) is present but proportionally lighter, and sits alongside hydrophobic films rather than instead of them.

The acidic buffering in A (citric acid/sodium citrate) also helps keep the cuticle lying flat.
    • **Net:** A pairs a moderately harsh wash with a near-complete humidity-resistant conditioning system; B pairs a gentler wash with conditioning but essentially no cuticle-sealing agents. For hair that swells and frizzes specifically in humidity, the hydrophobic film A deposits matters more than the extra mildness of B's base — hence A wins. Confidence is 4 rather than 5 only because A's sulfate detergency is a genuine, if manageable, drawback and B's cationic protein/polymers are not worthless for frizz.
