    • A pairs its polymer with humectants (Glycerin, Panthenol) that support the moisture side of the anti-frizz brief; B's moisture contribution is limited to amino acids and PPG-6 at trace-level positions.
    • Polyquaternium-10 and Ceramide NG are genuinely good anti-frizz actives and keep B in the running, but a cationic polymer at low level plus sparse small actives does not match A's layered film-forming and emollient deposition for humidity-swollen, frizz-prone hair.
