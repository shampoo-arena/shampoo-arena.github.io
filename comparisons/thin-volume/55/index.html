    • Formulation B runs on disodium laureth sulfosuccinate + lauryl betaine with only polyquaternium-10 as conditioning polymer — a genuinely lightweight, body-friendly combination.
    • Formulation B carries five hydrolysed proteins (soy, rice, pea, hazelnut, barley) plus glycine, glutamic acid and arginine — proteins and amino acids are the most relevant thickening/body actives for this profile.
    • Formulation B supports the scalp with panthenol, gluconolactone and capryloyl salicylic acid (gentle exfoliation/scalp conditioning) and a suite of hyaluronates for hydration.
    • Caveat: everything in B below roughly position 8 is ≤1%, so the long tail of seaweed extracts, multiple hyaluronic variants and biotin is likely largely token-level; this limits confidence rather than changing the direction of the verdict.
