    • Relative surfactant balance — A's system is spread across SLES + sulfosuccinate + cocamidopropyl betaine + amide, diluting the SLES share, whereas B is essentially SLES + salt + betaine with a hydrotrope (sodium xylenesulfonate) that adds cleansing power, not mildness
    • Minor SLS in A sits below the preservatives (≈≤0.5%), so it is not a significant harshness driver
    • Glyoxal (B) — an unnecessary, potentially irritating preservative species in a product for compromised skin barrier and porous hair; a mild negative
    • Hydroxypropyl methylcellulose (B) is a thickener with negligible conditioning value; histidine is trace-level and inconsequential
