    • Formulation B: Sodium C14-16 Olefin Sulfonate is still a strong anionic, but it is the only primary surfactant and is properly cushioned by Cocamidopropyl Betaine, Linoleamidopropyl PG-Dimonium Chloride Phosphate and several conditioners.
    • Formulation B: conditioning is via lighter, largely cationic materials (Polyquats 22/39, PEG-7 Amodimethicone — a self-limiting type) rather than a heavy dimethiconol film.
