    • Both contain a cationic slip agent for detangling (Guar Hydroxypropyltrimonium Chloride in A, Polyquaternium-7 in B) - roughly neutral between them.
    • A does offer genuine counterweights (Urea, PEG-8, Panthenol as humectants, Cocamidopropyl Betaine and Sodium Olivamphoacetate as mildness boosters), which keeps this from being a landslide, but it lacks any refatting lipid to offset its harsher surfactant system.
    • Neither formulation contains drying alcohols or non-water-soluble silicones, so those penalties do not apply.
