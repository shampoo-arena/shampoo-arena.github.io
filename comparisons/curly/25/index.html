But a stripping-but-bare cleanser is worse for this profile than a stronger cleanser with a real conditioning system: B would leave coily hair dry, tangled and undefined.
    • Glyoxal in B is an odd, reactive inclusion (a crosslinking/fixative-type molecule) with no benefit for this hair profile, and a potential irritant.
